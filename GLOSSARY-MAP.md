# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Homelab](GLOSSARY.md): the platform and what apps share: accounts, App access, the app contract
- [Notes](apps/notes/GLOSSARY.md): workspaces, teamspaces, pages and page access, as in Notion

## Relationships

- **Homelab → Notes**: a homelab admin's App access to Notes is a person's **Notes access**; it lets them into Notes, while workspace roles are Notes' own
